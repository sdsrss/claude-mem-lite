- 12b1bd30 (joined reading only) and v6.30.14 (as-written only) both erase it.

**Repro:** `/home/ai/.cache/tmp/rv-6315-r2/run.sh node /home/ai/.cache/tmp/rv-6315-r2/s/repro.mjs`. The minimal texts
